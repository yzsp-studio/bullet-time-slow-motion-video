## 🇬🇧 English

Starts from a script, storyboard, rough idea or reference material and produces a continuous, unified bullet-time short: an action beat triggers slow motion or a full time freeze, and the camera orbits the subject. It first collects requirements and creative direction; with only an idea in hand it offers 2-3 story directions and explains how bullet time serves the drama, then expands the chosen direction into a script and a storyboard draft covering shot size, camera move, transitions, duration, action beats and audio ownership. It then locks production specs, builds the storyboard and continuity map, creates character and scene asset anchors, and generates the orbit-freeze shots. Dialogue, narration, BGM and sound effects are produced as separate audio tracks, and the final cut is delivered only after review.

### ✨ What it does

- Four entry points — script, storyboard, idea or reference footage — all converge on one bullet-time short
- With only an idea, it proposes 2-3 story directions and says which dramatic beat slow motion serves
- Delivers a storyboard draft marking shot size, camera move, transition, duration, action beats and audio
- Locks style and production specs up front, with a storyboard and continuity map
- Builds character and scene asset anchors for consistent identity across multiple views
- Orbit-freeze shots plus separate dialogue, narration, BGM and SFX tracks, delivered after review
